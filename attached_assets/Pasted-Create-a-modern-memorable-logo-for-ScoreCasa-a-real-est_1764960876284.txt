Create a modern, memorable logo for ScoreCasa, a real estate scoring and analysis platform. The logo should communicate trust, growth, data-driven insights, and home ownership in a clean, professional manner.
Logo Composition
Icon Element

House Symbol: Simplified geometric house icon

Golden yellow/amber fill (#FFB800 or #FFC107)
Minimalist design with clean lines
Slight perspective or 3D effect (optional subtle shading)


Arrow Element: Upward trending arrow emerging from the house

Bright cyan/turquoise color (#00D9FF or #00B8D4)
Positioned at the top-right of the house, appearing to "break out" or extend from the roof
Symbolizes growth, upward trends, and increasing property value
Smooth, rounded arrow design with clean geometry



Typography

"Score": White or off-white (#FFFFFF or #F8F9FA)

Bold, modern sans-serif typeface (e.g., Inter Bold, Montserrat Bold, or DM Sans Bold)
Clean, professional letterforms


"Casa": Bright cyan matching the arrow (#00D9FF)

Same typeface and weight as "Score"
Creates visual connection between the arrow icon and brand name
"Casa" (Spanish for "home") reinforces the real estate focus



Layout

Icon positioned to the left
Wordmark positioned to the right of the icon
Optical alignment ensuring visual balance
Adequate spacing between icon and text (approximately 0.5x the icon width)
Single-line horizontal layout for primary usage

Color Palette

Primary Yellow: #FFB800 to #FFC107 (house icon)
Primary Cyan: #00D9FF to #00B8D4 (arrow and "Casa" text)
White: #FFFFFF ("Score" text)
Dark Background: #1a1530 or #0f0f1e (for primary usage on dark backgrounds)

Logo Variations Needed
Primary Logo

Full color on dark background (as shown)
Icon + full wordmark

Secondary Variations

White/monochrome version for light backgrounds
Icon-only version (square format for social media, favicons)
Stacked version (icon above text) for vertical layouts
All-white version for single-color applications

Style Guidelines
Design Principles

Modern & Tech-Forward: Clean lines, geometric shapes, contemporary typography
Trustworthy: Professional appearance, balanced composition
Optimistic: Upward arrow conveys positive momentum and growth
Accessible: Clear, legible at all sizes from favicon (16px) to billboard

Technical Specifications

Vector Format: Design in vector (SVG, AI, EPS) for infinite scalability
Minimum Size: Legible at 24px height
Clear Space: Maintain clear space around logo equal to the height of the house icon
Alignment: Optically centered and balanced

Icon Design Details
House Construction

Simple geometric house shape
Peaked roof with clean angles (approximately 45-60° roof pitch)
Square or rectangular base
No windows, doors, or excessive detail
Slightly rounded corners (1-2px radius) for a softer, modern feel

Arrow Construction

Smooth, flowing line with rounded ends
Arrow head at 45° angle
Stroke width proportional to house outline (if house has outline)
Appears to emerge naturally from the roof peak
Creates dynamic diagonal movement

Typography Specifications

Font Weight: Bold (700) or Semi-Bold (600)
Letter Spacing: Slightly tight (-0.02em to -0.04em) for modern feel
X-Height: Generous for readability
Recommended Fonts:

Inter Bold
Montserrat Bold
DM Sans Bold
Poppins SemiBold
Work Sans Bold

Professional: Trustworthy and credible in real estate market
